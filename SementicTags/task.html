Welcome to Mirai

Mirai School of Technology provides a modern, technology-focused learning environment. The college emphasizes coding, problem-solving and practical learning from an early stage. Students get opportunities to work on projects, develop technical skills and turn their ideas into real-world solutions. Its learning approach focuses on building a strong foundation in technology while encouraging creativity, experimentation and continuous improvement.

Learning Approach

Mirai follows a practical and technology-focused approach to education. Students learn through coding, problem-solving and project building instead of depending only on traditional classroom learning.

  • Learning through practical projects
  • Strong focus on coding and problem-solving
  • Building real-world applications
  • Teamwork and collaborative learning
  • Developing industry-relevant technical skills
  • Encouraging creativity and independent thinking
